## Limits and next step

This was a manual rental and a small CUDA correctness test. GPU identity was host-reported, not cryptographically attested. The test did not establish sustained search speed, large-range correctness, live GPU receipt integration, automated provider control, restart/watchdog behavior, or puzzle #71 coverage.

Next experiment: a larger controlled known-answer workload with sustained throughput measurement, then integration with the authenticated remote-worker lifecycle. No further rental is created by this record.
